# Score Energy Product Vision

## What the Product Is

Score Energy is a guided, gamified energy platform that helps people and organizations understand their electricity consumption, act on it, and improve over time.

The current product entry point is the electricity bill. After a user uploads a bill, Score Energy translates raw utility information into:

- understandable consumption insights
- practical next actions
- a visible score and progression system
- contextual guidance through a mascot assistant

The long-term product extends beyond individual bill analysis. Score Energy is intended to become the orchestrator of an ecosystem that connects users, energy consultants, and solution providers in a structured way.

## The Problem It Solves

Most users do not know how to interpret an electricity bill or what to do next after seeing high consumption.

Common problems include:

- bills are hard to read and full of technical language
- users do not know which actions are most relevant to their situation
- energy-saving advice is usually generic, disconnected, and easy to ignore
- improvement is difficult to measure over time
- access to qualified professionals and solution providers is fragmented

Score Energy addresses this by turning a confusing document into a guided improvement journey.

## Why It Matters

Energy consumption affects household budgets, business costs, and sustainability outcomes. Yet most people only interact with energy as a monthly charge, not as a system they can understand and actively improve.

This matters because:

- users need simple guidance, not technical reports
- small behavior changes can create recurring savings
- better awareness can unlock better decisions later
- trust is easier to build when advice is tied to the user's own bill
- a clear path from insight to action can create real economic and environmental impact

Score Energy matters when it reduces the gap between "I received my bill" and "I know what to do next."

## Why Gamification Is Essential

Gamification is not a cosmetic layer. It is a product mechanism for turning a low-engagement topic into a repeatable behavior loop.

Energy management usually fails as a consumer experience because it feels:

- abstract
- delayed
- boring
- punitive

Gamification makes the journey usable by adding:

- feedback through score and level progression
- reinforcement through visible improvement
- motivation through missions, milestones, and rewards
- continuity between one bill cycle and the next
- emotional connection through the mascot and guided journey

Without gamification, the product risks becoming a one-time utility checker. With gamification, it can become a habit-building platform.

## Why It Is More Than a Simple Energy Dashboard

A dashboard displays information. Score Energy is intended to guide decisions, shape behavior, and coordinate outcomes.

It is more than a dashboard because it must do all of the following:

- interpret the bill in plain language
- explain what matters now versus later
- prioritize next actions instead of showing raw data only
- create continuity across months through score evolution
- use the mascot as an active guide before and after analysis
- connect users to human experts and market solutions when needed

The product should therefore be designed as a journey engine, not a reporting surface.

## Product Principles

Future engineering work should preserve these principles:

1. Start from user understanding, not energy jargon.
2. Use the bill as the anchor for trust and personalization.
3. Convert analysis into clear next actions.
4. Make progress visible over time.
5. Treat gamification as core product logic, not decoration.
6. Keep the user journey simple in the MVP while building toward the ecosystem vision.
